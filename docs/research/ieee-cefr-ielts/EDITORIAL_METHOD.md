# Specificity and clarity audit for the two IEEE-style manuscripts

The writing follows two complementary editorial principles:

1. **Specific knowledge before fluency.** A claim needs a traceable observation, scholarly source, or explicitly labeled hypothesis. The writer's personal experience must never be invented. This follows the specificity diagnostic shared by the user.
2. **Clear technical meaning.** Name the actor, use an ordinary verb, keep terminology stable, define domain terms, avoid stacked hedges, and preserve scope qualifiers. These principles are adapted from the STE-*flavored* mode of `asd-ste100-skill` rather than strict aerospace controlled-language certification.

## Applied rewrites

| Generic / ambiguous wording | Evidence-backed writing adopted in the manuscript | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Stella revolutionizes language learning." | "`Reading.tsx` currently samples six related example sentences; this does not establish comprehension across paragraphs." | Replaces marketing language with a code-observable behavior and a bounded inference. |
| "Extensive reading raises IELTS scores." | "A 2025 meta-analysis reported an average immediate effect of d=0.38 across its included interventions; no specific IELTS-band gain is implied." | Retains sample, outcome, and generalization limits. |
| "FSRS measures reading mastery." | "FSRS schedules word recall; independent reading comprehension requires separate, unseen passages and skill-level evidence." | Names two different constructs. |
| "Our new AI system produces accurate explanations." | "Every question should carry reviewed evidence IDs; generated explanations remain drafts until checked." | Does not assert unmeasured implementation success. |
| "Users improved after using Stella." | "The proposed evaluation will test transfer to previously unseen, difficulty-matched passages." | The study has not taken place. |

## Terminology policy

- **Passage:** a coherent text with paragraph/sentence identifiers; not a shuffled collection of example sentences.
- **Attempt:** an answer event, including assistance and first-response provenance.
- **Independent comprehension:** performance on unseen tasks without hints or answer exposure.
- **CEFR-oriented profile:** evidence relative to reading can-do descriptors, not a label automatically inherited from a vocabulary list.
- **IELTS readiness:** performance on relevant practice tasks and time constraints, not an official IELTS result.
- **Learning transfer:** performance on new content; not repeated accuracy on memorized questions.

## Bilingual consistency checks

- Same 12 high-level sections and three tables.
- Same three mathematical expressions (selection heuristic, smoothed skill estimate, intervention estimand).
- Same 17 cited sources in the same numbered order.
- Study quantities: 73 studies, 82 interventions, d=0.38; 42 studies, N=3,802; Schmitt's 661 participants; Cepeda's 317 experiments; IELTS 3 sections, 40 questions, 60 minutes, 2,150–2,750 words.
- Same explicit limitation: no learner trial and no proven gains for Stella.

**Final rule:** delete a sentence if it neither supplies evidence, explains a causal/design mechanism, identifies a trade-off, nor states a testable proposition.
